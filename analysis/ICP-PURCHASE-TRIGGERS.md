## Flagship offer hypothesis

Test Agent Wall as a useful free directory first. A basic profile is free. A finite **Founding 100** placement is a clearly labeled sponsorship, initially limited to a 25-slot validation tranche at a proposed $99 one-time price; this is a test price, not approved live pricing or evidence of willingness to pay. Sponsorship never buys organic rank or “verification.” Complimentary anchor profiles are labeled. Recurring verified/pro tiers are deferred until traffic, qualified outbound clicks, operational verification cost, and buyer retention are observed.

## Validation gates

Before live charging: recruit qualified vendors without implying demand; test the value proposition and objections; instrument profile/outbound behavior; define a minimum service term rather than “forever”; publish moderation, edit, takedown, cancellation and refund rules; and obtain explicit operator approval for exact prices and activation. Reject the concept if buyers primarily want undisclosed rank, qualified discovery does not materialize, or verification cost exceeds plausible gross margin.
