## Crawl and quality controls

- Use crawlable category → collection → profile links. Google recommends linking important pages through site navigation rather than relying on search boxes: [ecommerce site structure guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/help-google-understand-your-ecommerce-site-structure).
- Canonicalize stable editorial pages. Keep arbitrary filters, empty combinations, internal search results, and sub-threshold collections out of the index; Google warns that faceted URLs can create effectively infinite crawl spaces: [faceted navigation guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/crawling-managing-faceted-navigation).
- A finite tile alone is not an indexable page. Sponsorship changes presentation, never the evidence threshold or organic rank.
- Recheck destination and product evidence; show `last tested`; noindex or remove stale/unsafe pages according to policy.

## Falsification conditions

Agent Wall loses the SEO lead if fewer than five genuinely relevant agents can support most intended collections, integration/capability claims cannot be verified economically, benchmark refresh cannot be sustained, or indexed profiles collapse into repeated vendor copy. Search Console impressions and qualified outbound clicks—not page count—must decide whether the surface expands.
