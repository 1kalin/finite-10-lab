# Product Guide

## Product promise

Finite, visible internet inventory with a clear buyer benefit and transparent availability.

## Prototype semantics

- Reservations are simulations and never imply ownership or payment.
- A tile is a licensed placement right, not real property or an investment.
- Live versions require moderation, refund/takedown rules, link safety, and payment-webhook authority.

## Success measures

Qualified reservation intent, share rate, repeat visits, outbound clicks, moderation burden, and willingness to pay.
