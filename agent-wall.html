Before you decide

Straight answers

What does $100 mean?

It is a proposed one-time launch price for one placement. Checkout is disabled; exact live pricing and terms require approval before payments open.

Does placement mean endorsement?

No. Placement, verification, editorial inclusion, ownership, and ranking are separate. We will label each state rather than imply trust.

What happens to my data?

This prototype does not ask for an account or collect payment details. A live submission flow must publish its privacy, moderation, update, removal, and refund rules before launch.

Can I reserve now?

No. The interaction below is a preview only. It creates no hold, ownership right, scarcity signal, or obligation.
