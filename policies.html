Agent Wall
Trust and policies

Clear rules before anything goes live.

Last updated 25 August 2026. Agent Wall is currently a prototype: submissions, reservations, accounts, checkout, and payments are disabled.

Terms

Using the prototype lets you explore an illustrative directory and placement preview. It does not create a contract, reservation, hold, ownership interest, licence, purchase, or obligation. Demo tiles and example profiles are not customers or evidence of demand.

A future paid placement would be a revocable licence to display an eligible listing, not real property, an investment, verification, endorsement, traffic guarantee, or ranking advantage. Final commercial terms, service period, price, and operator identity will be published before checkout is enabled.

Privacy notice

The current prototype does not request accounts, submissions, payment details, or personal information. It uses no form that sends data to Agent Wall. Normal hosting infrastructure may process basic request data such as IP address, browser details, requested page, and timestamp for security and reliability.

Before analytics, submissions, accounts, or payments are activated, this notice will identify the operator, lawful purposes, providers, retention periods, user rights, and a working privacy contact.

Listing standards

  • Listings must describe a real, reachable product or service with accurate claims and a safe HTTPS destination.
  • Evidence, verification, commercial placement, and editorial inclusion remain separate and visibly labelled.
  • No impersonation, malware, deceptive redirects, unlawful content, fabricated traction, or unsupported performance claims.
  • Owner edits return to review. Only approved, public listings may appear.

Refunds and cancellation

No payment can currently be made, so there is nothing to cancel or refund. Previewing a tile creates no hold or charge.

Before paid checkout opens, Agent Wall will publish the exact price, service period, cancellation method, refund eligibility and timing, consequences of rejection or takedown, and any non-refundable work. Those live terms will govern instead of this prototype disclosure.

Moderation and takedown

Future submissions will enter review and remain unpublished until approved. Agent Wall may reject, hide, or remove listings that breach the standards, become unsafe or unreachable, or create legal or user-safety risk. Decisions retain a reason and audit history; material owner edits require another review.

A live reporting channel and appeal/correction process will be published before public submissions open. Urgent legal and safety reports will be prioritised. Removal of a listing does not erase required audit, fraud-prevention, or legal records.

Contact

Agent Wall is not accepting submissions, purchases, support requests, or legal notices during this prototype phase. A verified operator name and working contact channel will appear here before any of those flows open. We will not invent an unattended support address merely to make the prototype look live.
